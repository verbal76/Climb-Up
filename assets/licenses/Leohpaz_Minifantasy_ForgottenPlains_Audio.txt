How Upwardly uses it: the audio files are NOT stored in the source repository. The owner's copy sits on a DRAFT release ("Sfx") of the
repository, visible only to collaborators and to build jobs holding a contents: write token. The build workflows convert 20 of the 37 clips
(footsteps on grass/dirt/stone, landings on grass/stone, hits on wood/brick, three ambience loops) into small, trimmed, loudness-normalised mono
OGG files that ship inside the built game only. The game is credited on its Credits screen. The pack's music tracks and the patron-only
exclusive sounds are not used.
