Minifantasy - Forgotten Plains Audio Pack
By Leohpaz (with Krishna Palacio)
https://leohpaz.itch.io/minifantasy-forgotten-plains-sfx-pack

Summary of the author's terms as shown on the pack's page (this is a note, not the pack and not the author's full text):
  - May be used in personal and commercial projects.
  - The pack itself may NOT be sold or redistributed, even for free.
  - Credit is not mandatory but is appreciated.

How Upwardly uses it: the audio files are NOT stored in the source repository. The build workflows fetch the owner's licensed copy at
build time (tools/ci/fetch_pack_sfx.sh), convert the few clips the game uses (footsteps, landings, wood and brick hits, three ambience loops)
to small OGG files, and ship them inside the built game only. If the pack is missing, the game falls back to its built-in sounds.
